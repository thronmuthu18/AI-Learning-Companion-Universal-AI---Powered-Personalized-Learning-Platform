# UNIVERSAL GOAL IDENTIFICATION (Phase 09)

## Scope Across All Domains
AI Learning Companion supports goal creation across **all 30 universal domains** in `DomainRegistry` (`@alc/learning-engine`) as well as custom runtime-registered domains:
- Academic coursework (Physics, Mathematics, Law, Biology, Engineering)
- Competitive and public examinations (GATE ECE/CS, UPSC, GRE, Aptitude)
- Professional & business skills (Business Accounting, Finance, Management, Design, Vocational, Software, AI/ML)
- Personal growth (Languages & Communication, Music, Creative Arts)

Zero domains (including Full Stack Development) act as hidden defaults.

## UI Entry Paths (Web & Mobile)
1. **Multi-Domain Pathway Templates (`GOAL_TEMPLATES`)** across engineering, science, accounting, competitive exams, software, AI/ML, academic study, and custom goals.
2. **Searchable Universal Domain & Subject Explorer** surfacing domain content coverage status (`available`, `partial`, `planned`) transparently.
3. **Natural-Language Free-Text Entry** routed through the structured interpretation and clarification flow before creating goals.
